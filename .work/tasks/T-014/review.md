[MEDIUM] src/voiceroom/segmenter.py:100-102 (+ _begin:116-117, _end:124-128): The max-turn split ignores the silence/hangover state and always starts a new turn on the next frame. → (a) A speaker can stop talking within about 400 ms before the 15 s cap, so the split fires during the hangover. The segmenter then emits `SpeechStart` plus a phantom `TurnEnd` with `t_start == t_end` and audio that is pure silence. Repro: `max_turn_s=1.0`, speech up to the cap, then silence. The output is `TurnEnd(0.992, 0.992, 1536 samples)`. In T-016 this silent "turn" would be identified and could be committed to the provider, which then generates a reply to nothing. The spurious `SpeechStart` could also trigger barge-in. (b) `_begin` sets `_last_speech_idx = start_idx - 1` and `_frames = []`, so `keep_to - first == tail`. With `tail_s=0`, the phantom turn crashes with `np.concatenate([])` ValueError mid-stream. With default settings, `flush()` called immediately after a split (the stream ends on the split frame) crashes the same way ("need at least one array to concatenate"). → Fix: on max-turn, if `_silence > 0` (or the current frame is non-speech), end the turn normally and go idle without calling `_begin`. Only continue into a new turn when the current frame is speech. In `_end`, return no event (or guard against it) when no speech frame falls inside the turn. Never call `np.concatenate` on an empty list.

[MEDIUM] src/voiceroom/segmenter.py:89,95-97: `_speech` counts every frame since onset, including non-speech frames inside the hangover. `IdentifyReady` therefore fires after 1.0 s of wall time, not after 1.0 s of speech. → Consider a speaker who talks in short bursts with pauses shorter than 400 ms ("yes... ok... sure"). Repro: a 3-on/11-off frame pattern gives `IdentifyReady` at 1.02 s of audio that contains only 0.29 s of speech. `MIN_DECISION_S` (config.py:20-22) was calibrated in T-012 on segment length of continuous LibriSpeech speech, where shorter segments already have about 19 % FRR. So T-016's early decision would run the matcher on far less voiced audio than the threshold assumes, which inflates FR and FA on exactly the early path. → Fix: keep a separate counter of speech frames (prob >= threshold) and gate `IdentifyReady` on it. Keep `_speech` (frames since onset) only for the max-turn cap.

[LOW] scripts/smoke/smoke_segmenter.py:48-49: The `IdentifyReady` check asserts `len(r.audio) >= 1.0 s`. That length includes the pre-roll and any silence, so it passes even in the sparse-speech scenario above. The comment says "before the turn ends", but nothing checks that ordering. → A regression that fires `IdentifyReady` on mostly silence, or after `TurnEnd`, passes the smoke check. → Fix: assert that each `IdentifyReady` appears in the event list before its `TurnEnd`. Once the counter above is fixed, also assert the speech-frame count (or VAD-positive duration) behind it is >= 1.0 s.

[LOW] scripts/smoke/smoke_segmenter.py:54-59: The split check says "with continuity (no lost audio)" but never verifies continuity. It also feeds pure speech, so the split never lands during a pause or at the end of speech, which is where the MEDIUM bug above lives. → The phantom-turn and empty-concatenate crash pass `make check`. → Fix: add a case where speech stops 1-5 frames before `max_turn_s`, then call `flush()` right at the split frame. Assert that no `TurnEnd` has `t_end <= t_start` and that nothing raises. Optionally assert that consecutive split turns abut (`next.t_start == prev.t_end`).

[LOW] src/voiceroom/segmenter.py:57: `round(max_turn_s / FRAME_S)` fails for `max_turn_s=math.inf`, which is the natural way to say "disable splitting": it raises `OverflowError` at construction. → A pipeline config that disables the cap crashes on startup. → Fix: accept `None` or `inf` to mean no cap (`self.max_frames = None` and skip the check), or validate the argument with a clear error.
