[MEDIUM] src/voiceroom/rooms/base.py:97: `played = min(_pub_s, now - _start)` assumes the audio was published without gaps. If the provider stalls mid-reply (common with a network provider), wall time counts silence as played audio. Reproduced: 0.5 s published at t=0, a 1.5 s stall, then a 3 s burst, interrupt at t=2.0 → reports 2000 ms when 1000 ms actually played. → Failure: OpenAI truncates the assistant item at 2000 ms, so the model believes the room heard words nobody heard. That breaks the acceptance requirement "with the ms actually published". → Fix: `played = _pub_s - max(0.0, _end - now)` (`_end` already tracks the gaps correctly), clamped to be at least 0.
